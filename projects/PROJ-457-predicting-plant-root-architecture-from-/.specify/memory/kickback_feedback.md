## Unresolved concerns

- The two‑stage imputation policy (species‑level mean then k‑NN) may introduce systematic bias in nutrient values, compromising construct validity of the predictor variables. A bias‑assessment is listed (T027) but the plan does not specify how the bias will be quantified or corrected.
- Environmental covariates (soil_moisture, temperature, elevation) are included only *when present* (FR‑026). Their optional nature creates a risk of omitted‑variable confounding, as nutrient effects could be partially mediated by these factors. The plan lacks a strategy to enforce inclusion or to model missing covariates.
- A priori power analysis targets a medium effect size (Cohen f² = 0.15) and a per‑species minimum of 30 observations. If true effects are smaller, the study may be under‑powered, yet the plan does not provide a contingency (e.g., increasing sample size or aggregating across species) nor does it assess overall power across all species combined.
- Excluding species with < 30 observations (T011) can induce selection bias, especially if low‑sample species differ systematically in nutrient‑trait relationships. The only bias check is leave‑one‑species‑out (T026), which does not capture bias introduced by the initial exclusion step.
- The merging of root phenotype and soil nutrient records relies on a ‘verified merged dataset’ but the plan does not describe the matching logic (e.g., spatial/temporal tolerance). Mis‑alignment could lead to measurement error in the nutrient predictors, threatening construct validity.
- Functional Requirement FR‑021 (retrieve peer‑reviewed literature values for expected nutrient effect sizes) is not addressed by any task in the implementation plan. No task lists FR‑021 in its “FR / SC Covered” column, nor is there an explicit step to retrieve and store literature effect‑size values.
- The plan relies on a single "verified merged dataset" (e.g., `blanchon/merged_dataset`) as the primary source of both root phenotype and soil nutrient data. However, the functional requirement FR‑001 explicitly mandates downloading **separate** root phenotype datasets (RootReader, PlantPheno) and soil nutrient data (ISRIC‑World Soil Information) and then merging them. The merged dataset cited has not been demonstrated to contain the required root‑trait fields and soil‑nutrient columns, nor is there a verification step confirming its suitability. This creates a mismatch between the dataset strategy and the specification, risking an inappropriate data source for the research question.
