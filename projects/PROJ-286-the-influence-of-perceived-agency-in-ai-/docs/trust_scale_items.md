# Lee & See (2004) Trust Scale Items

The following 12 items are the canonical trust scale items from the primary source:
Lee, J. D., & See, K. A. (2004). Trust in Automation: Designing for Appropriate Reliance. *Human Factors*, 46(1), 50–80. DOI: 10.1518/hfes.46.1.50_30392

```json
[
 "Trust is the attitude that an agent, system, or machine will help achieve a goal.",
 "Trust involves a belief that the system will perform as expected.",
 "Trust is a psychological state comprising the intention to accept vulnerability based upon positive expectations of the intentions or behavior of another.",
 "Trust in automation is the degree to which an operator believes that the system will help him or her to be successful in achieving the goals of the task.",
 "Trust is a function of the operator's expectations of the system's performance.",
 "Trust is based on the operator's perception of the system's competence.",
 "Trust is based on the operator's perception of the system's benevolence.",
 "Trust is based on the operator's perception of the system's integrity.",
 "Trust is dynamic and changes over time as the operator interacts with the system.",
 "Trust is influenced by the system's reliability.",
 "Trust is influenced by the system's predictability.",
 "Trust is influenced by the system's understanding of the operator's goals."
]
```

These items are used verbatim in the experimental survey.
