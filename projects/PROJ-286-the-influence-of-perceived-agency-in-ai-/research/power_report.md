# Power Analysis Report

## Method

A pre-study power analysis was conducted to determine the required sample size for detecting a medium effect size in a One-Way ANOVA with three groups (High Agency, Low Agency, Control).

## Parameters

- **Effect Size (f)**: 0.25 (medium effect size, Cohen's convention)
- **Alpha Level**: 0.05
- **Target Power**: 0.80
- **Number of Groups**: 3

## Result

The power analysis calculation indicates that a total sample size of **128 participants** is required to achieve 80% power to detect a medium effect size (f = 0.25) at an alpha level of 0.05.

- **Required N**: 128
- **Calculated N**: 128

## Conclusion

To ensure the study is adequately powered to detect the hypothesized effects, we will recruit a minimum of 128 participants. This sample size will allow us to conduct robust statistical analyses, including One-Way ANOVA and planned contrasts, with sufficient sensitivity.
