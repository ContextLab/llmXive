# Pre-Study Power Analysis Report

## Study Design
This report details the power analysis conducted to determine the required sample size for the study on "The Influence of Perceived Agency in AI Interactions on Trust". The primary analysis will utilize a One-Way ANOVA to compare trust scores across three experimental conditions: High Agency, Low Agency, and Control.

## Parameters
| Parameter | Value | Description |
|:--- |:--- |:--- |
| **Effect Size (Cohen's f)** | 0.25 | Medium effect size based on literature review |
| **Significance Level (α)** | 0.05 | Probability of Type I error |
| **Target Power (1-β)** | 0.8 | Probability of correctly rejecting null hypothesis |
| **Number of Groups (k)** | 3 | High Agency, Low Agency, Control |
| **Statistical Test** | One-Way ANOVA (F-test) | Analysis of Variance |

## Results
- **Required Sample Size per Group**: 53 participants
- **Total Required Sample Size**: 159 participants
- **Analysis Method**: One-Way ANOVA (F-test)
- **Software**: statsmodels

## Conclusion
To achieve a statistical power of **0.8** for detecting a medium effect size (0.25) at a significance level of 0.05 using a One-Way ANOVA with 3 groups, the study requires a minimum of **53 participants per condition**, totaling **159 participants**.

This sample size ensures that the study is adequately powered to detect the hypothesized differences in trust scores between the experimental conditions.
