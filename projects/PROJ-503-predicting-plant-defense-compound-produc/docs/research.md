## 4. Integration Notes

- **Pairing Logic**: Samples are paired based on `biosample_id` or exact biological replicate identifiers found in the metadata, not just condition labels.
- **Format**: Raw counts from GEO are normalized to TPM in `code/create_expression_matrix.py`. Metabolite concentrations are log2-transformed in `code/create_metabolite_matrix.py`.
- **Versioning**: All dataset versions and access dates are recorded in `data/sources.yaml` as per Task T016.
