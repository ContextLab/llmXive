# Research: Phenomenological AI: First-Person Experience Modeling in Language Models

## Overview

This research investigates how prompting strategies influence the structural coherence of first-person phenomenological reports generated by Large Language Models (LLMs). The study employs a computational pipeline to generate reports, compute validity metrics, and perform statistical analysis, adhering to strict reproducibility and data hygiene standards.

## Dataset Strategy

The study does not rely on external pre-existing datasets. Instead, it generates its own primary dataset (the "Phenomenological Corpus") using open-source LLMs. The validity of the study depends on the quality of the generation and the rigor of the metrics.

**Primary Data Source (Generated)**:
-   **Source**: `meta-llama/Llama-7b-chat-hf` and `mistralai/Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.2` (via HuggingFace).
-   **Access**: Programmatic download via `transformers` and `llama-cpp-python` (GGUF).
-   **Justification**: These models are open-source, accessible without rate limits (via HuggingFace Hub), and support CPU inference with quantization. They are standard baselines for LLM research.
-   **Verification**: The models are available on HuggingFace Hub. The plan uses the `Q4_K` quantization (Verified Facts: 2606.12280, https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12280) for CPU feasibility.

**No Access-Gated Data**: The study explicitly avoids datasets requiring credentials (e.g., ADNI, HCP) as they cannot be fetched by the CI runner. The entire dataset is generated on the fly.

## Model & Method Selection

### LLM Inference
-   **Method**: CPU-only inference using GGUF quantization.
-   **Rationale**: The GitHub Actions free-tier lacks GPU. Full-precision 7B models exceed RAM limits. `Q4_K` quantization reduces memory footprint to ~4-5 GB, allowing inference on the 7 GB RAM box.
-   **Source**: The use of `Q4_K` for local execution is supported by the **Verified Facts** block (source: arXiv 2606.12280).

### Validity Metrics

1.  **Logical Coherence (Secondary/Exploratory)**:
    -   **Method**: Natural Language Inference (NLI) model (e.g., `roberta-large-mnli`) applied pairwise to sentences within a report.
    -   **Aggregation**: For reports with ≥2 sentences, the score is the **mean of pairwise contradiction probabilities** (1 - P(contradiction)). For reports with <2 sentences, the score is 1.0 (trivially consistent) and flagged.
    -   **Rationale**: NLI models are trained to detect logical contradictions. This measures "Logical Coherence" (alignment with NLI training data), not "Phenomenological Consistency". It is treated as a secondary metric to avoid category errors.
    -   **Source**: Standard NLI models (e.g., from HuggingFace) are widely used for coherence evaluation.

2.  **Output Reproducibility (Primary Control)**:
    -   **Method**: Cosine similarity between embeddings of **repeated generations** (5 repeats) for the same prompt/seed (using `sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2`).
    -   **Aggregation**: The mean similarity across multiple repeats is calculated per prompt. This aggregated score is used as the "Output Reproducibility" metric.
    -   **Rationale**: This measures the model's inherent **stochasticity** (variance in output for the same prompt). It is not a measure of "phenomenological stability" but a control variable to assess model reliability. High variance indicates the prompt strategy is unstable.
    -   **Source**: Sentence Transformers are the standard for semantic similarity tasks.

3.  **Marker Specificity (Primary Phenomenological)**:
    -   **Method**: Rule-based keyword dictionary for sensory, temporal, and intentional markers.
    -   **Metric**: **Marker Specificity** = (Count of Targeted Markers) / (Total Markers).
    -   **Rationale**: Raw counts are tautological (prompts ask for markers). The ratio normalizes for this, measuring if the model successfully generates *targeted* markers relative to total output. This distinguishes "phenomenological depth" from "keyword stuffing".
    -   **Source**: Dictionary derived from phenomenological literature (e.g., Husserl, Merleau-Ponty) and validated against qualitative research conventions.

### Statistical Analysis

-   **Method**: **Linear Mixed-Effects Model (LMM)** with 'Prompt ID' and 'Model ID' as random intercepts, and 'Strategy' as a fixed effect.
-   **Rationale**: The data has a nested structure (multiple generations per prompt). Standard ANOVA violates independence (pseudoreplication). Crucially, the inclusion of 'Model ID' as a random effect controls for architectural differences between Llama-7B and Mistral-7B, preventing the confounding of strategy effects with model-specific biases.
-   **Aggregation**: 'Output Reproducibility' is aggregated (mean) per prompt before entering the LMM.
-   **Correction**: Benjamini-Hochberg FDR correction for multiple comparisons.
-   **Source**: Standard statistical practice in computational linguistics and psychology for nested data.

## Statistical Power & Sample Size

-   **Pilot Study (N=20 per condition)**: Due to the 6-hour compute limit, the initial run uses N=20.
-   **Power Analysis**: A post-hoc power analysis will be conducted for N=20.
    -   **Risk**: High risk of Type II error (β > 0.5 for medium effects).
    -   **Mitigation**: The study will explicitly report "underpowered to detect medium effects" if no significant difference is found. The Pilot serves to estimate effect sizes for the Full Study (N=80) planning.
    -   **Full Study**: If the Pilot shows promising effect sizes, the Full Study (N=80) will be attempted via GPU offload (Kaggle) or accepted as a limitation.

## Phenomenological Validity Justification

The study addresses the concern that "first-person" is a category error for machines by focusing on **structural coherence** rather than ontological experience. The metrics are justified as follows:

-   **Logical Coherence**: A coherent report must not contradict itself logically. NLI provides an objective measure of this (as a secondary metric).
-   **Output Reproducibility**: A stable report should not vary wildly across repeated generations. Embedding similarity quantifies model stochasticity (as a control).
-   **Marker Specificity**: Phenomenological reports are defined by specific linguistic features. The ratio metric avoids the "self-fulfilling prophecy" by normalizing for prompt instructions.

The study treats the findings as **associational**, not causal, due to the observational nature of the prompting manipulation. This aligns with the assumption in the spec.

## Human Qualitative Validation

-   **Method**: Two independent philosophy graduate students rate a subset of reports per condition for coherence.
-   **Metric**: Cohen's κ for inter-rater reliability.
-   **Threshold**: κ ≥ 0.6 (moderate agreement) as per Landis & Koch (1977).
-   **Justification**: Human judgment is essential to ground automated metrics in phenomenological validity. The study includes a sensitivity analysis on the κ threshold (FR-011).

## Risks & Limitations

-   **Compute Limits**: CPU inference may be slow. Mitigation: Use `Q4_K` quantization and limit samples to Pilot (N=20).
-   **Model Bias**: LLMs may exhibit biases in their generations. Mitigation: Use multiple models and strategies to diversify; control for model ID in statistical analysis.
-   **Human Rater Availability**: Raters may not be available. Mitigation: Plan for remote rating and use automated metrics as primary.
-   **Validity of Metrics**: Automated metrics may not fully capture phenomenological depth. Mitigation: Use sensitivity analysis and human validation to cross-check.
-   **Statistical Power**: N=20 may be insufficient. Mitigation: Explicitly report power limitations and use Pilot for Full Study planning.

## Conclusion

The research plan is feasible on a CPU-only environment using GGUF quantization. The metrics are grounded in established NLP and phenomenological literature, with explicit adjustments to avoid category errors (Logical Coherence vs. Phenomenological Consistency) and tautologies (Marker Specificity). The study prioritizes reproducibility and data hygiene, ensuring all artifacts are checksummed and seeds are pinned. The statistical design explicitly controls for model architecture to ensure valid comparisons of prompting strategies.
