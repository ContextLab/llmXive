## Introduction
You are invited to participate in a research study investigating how visual crowding affects the ability to recognize facial emotions. This study involves viewing images of faces with varying levels of visual clutter and identifying the emotion displayed.

## Purpose of the Study
The purpose of this research is to understand the mechanisms of visual perception and emotion recognition under crowded conditions. This knowledge may contribute to better understanding of visual processing in various populations.

## Procedures
If you agree to participate, you will be asked to:
1. Read and sign this consent form.
2. Complete a brief demographic questionnaire.
3. View a series of facial images under different visual crowding conditions.
4. Identify the emotion displayed in each image from a list of options.
5. Complete the session in approximately 20-30 minutes.

## Risks and Discomforts
The risks associated with this study are minimal. You may experience minor eye strain or fatigue from viewing the images. You may stop the session at any time without penalty.

## Benefits
There are no direct benefits to you as a participant. However, the knowledge gained may contribute to the scientific understanding of visual perception and emotion recognition.

## Confidentiality
Your data will be kept confidential. All responses will be anonymized using a unique participant ID. No personally identifiable information will be published. Data will be stored on secure servers accessible only to the research team.

## Voluntary Participation
Your participation in this study is entirely voluntary. You may choose not to participate or may withdraw at any time without penalty or loss of benefits to which you are otherwise entitled.
