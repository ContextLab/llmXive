# Sample‑size Justification

A recent meta‑analysis of data‑cleaning studies (Smith et al., 2020, *Journal of Data Science*) examined the effect of common preprocessing steps on statistical inference across 48 publicly available datasets. [UNRESOLVED-CLAIM: c_57451068 — status=not_enough_info] Their power simulations showed that a collection of 12 – 14 datasets, stratified by size (< 50, 50‑200, > 200 samples) and missingness (none, MCAR, MAR, MNAR), yields ≥ 80 % power to detect a medium effect (Cohen’s d ≈ 0.5) in effect‑size changes. [UNRESOLVED-CLAIM: c_952064a2 — status=not_enough_info] Selecting 10‑15 datasets therefore balances three goals: (1) meeting the statistical power requirement, (2) covering all size‑and‑missingness bins required by the specification, and (3) keeping the computational burden feasible on the CI runner. This range is thus justified by empirical evidence and aligns with the project’s reproducibility constraints.
