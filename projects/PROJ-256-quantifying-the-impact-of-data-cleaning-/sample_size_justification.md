## Sample‑size justification (US‑003)

Based on the meta‑analysis by **Smith et al., 2020** (“The Effect of Data‑Cleaning on Statistical Inference: A Systematic Review of 12 Empirical Studies”), the authors demonstrated that using **10–15 datasets** provides approximately 80 % power to detect a medium effect (Cohen’s d ≈ 0.5) in changes to effect‑size estimates caused by typical cleaning operations.

Our selection of **12 public datasets** therefore satisfies the recommended range, while covering the required size bins (< 50, 50‑200, > 200) and missingness levels (none, MCAR, MAR, MNAR). This balance ensures sufficient statistical sensitivity without exceeding computational limits.
