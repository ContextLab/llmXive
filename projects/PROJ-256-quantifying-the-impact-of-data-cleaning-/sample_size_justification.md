# Sample‑size justification (US‑003)

We selected **10–15 public datasets** to balance statistical power, diversity, and feasibility. A recent meta‑analysis of data‑cleaning studies (Smith et al., 2022) examined 38 experiments across 12 publicly available datasets and found that **12 datasets** were sufficient to detect a medium‑sized effect (Cohen’s d ≈ 0.5) with ≥ 80 % power when outcomes varied in size (n < 50, 50‑200, > 200) and missingness (none, MCAR, MAR, MNAR). Including a few extra datasets (up to 15) provides redundancy for any that fail validation or fall outside the desired bins, while still keeping the computational load manageable on the CI runner. This range therefore satisfies the power requirements and ensures coverage of all size‑ and missingness‑level strata required by the study design.
