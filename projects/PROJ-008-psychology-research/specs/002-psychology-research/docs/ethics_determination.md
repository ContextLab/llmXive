# Ethics Determination: Exempt Status

## Determination
**Status**: Exempt from IRB review

## Justification
This project conducts a secondary analysis of de-identified public registry data
from ClinicalTrials.gov and the Open Science Framework (OSF). Per 45 CFR 46.104(d),
research involving the use of existing datasets that are publicly available and
do not contain identifiable private information is exempt from IRB oversight.

## Data Sources
- **ClinicalTrials.gov**: Public registry of clinical trial results; all data is
 de-identified and publicly accessible.
- **Open Science Framework (OSF)**: Open repository for research materials; all
 included studies are publicly shared with consent for secondary use.

## Privacy Considerations
- No direct contact with study participants
- No access to identifiable private information
- All data is aggregated at the study level (no individual participant data)
- Registry data is de-identified by design

## Compliance
This determination aligns with:
- 45 CFR 46.104(d) (Exempt Research Categories)
- Institutional Policy on Secondary Data Analysis
- Constitution Principle III (Ethical Compliance)

## Limitations
- This determination applies only to secondary analysis of registry data
- If primary data collection is added, a full IRB protocol would be required
- Data usage must comply with registry terms of service
