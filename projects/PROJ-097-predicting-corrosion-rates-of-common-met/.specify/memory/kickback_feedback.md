## Unresolved concerns

- The empirical p‑value is defined as the proportion of shuffled ΔRMSE ≤ observed ("empirical p‑value = proportion of shuffled ΔRMSE ≤ observed"). For permutation‑importance the natural null is that shuffling destroys predictive signal, so the observed importance (increase in RMSE) should be compared to the *upper* tail of the null distribution (proportion of shuffled ΔRMSE ≥ observed). Using the ≤ direction inverts the test, potentially labeling non‑important interactions as significant and vice‑versa.
