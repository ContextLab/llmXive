# Analysis Summary

This document summarizes the key findings, limitations, and results of the study on the influence of emotional contagion on collective decision-making.

## Data Coverage

The analysis is based on real data collected from online forums. The dataset includes threads from multiple subreddits and Stack Exchange sites, filtered for sufficient seed posts and metadata completeness.

## Statistical Power and Sampling Analysis

### Issues Detected
- **LOW_SAMPLE_SIZE** (HIGH): Total sample size (N) is below the recommended threshold of 100 for robust statistical power.
- **LOW_GROUND_TRUTH_RATIO** (MEDIUM): Valid ground truth threads are below the 30% threshold.

### Recommendations
- Interpret results with caution due to limited sample size.
- Results relying on ground truth validation may have limited generalizability.

### Conclusion
The study has limited statistical power. Results should be interpreted with appropriate caution regarding the identified limitations.

## Limitations
This study is observational with no random assignment. All reported relationships between emotional contagion and decision quality are correlational and should not be interpreted as causal.

## Power Limitation Notice
Power limitation detected: n < 100 threads. Results should be interpreted with caution due to limited statistical power.
