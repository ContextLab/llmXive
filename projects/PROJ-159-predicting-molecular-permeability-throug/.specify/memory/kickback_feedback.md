## Unresolved concerns

- The plan assumes the existence of a peer‑reviewed experimental permeability dataset (FR‑016) but the research document flags that no such dataset is currently available, causing the pipeline to abort. Without a valid target variable the core research question (predicting permeability) cannot be answered.
- The power analysis assumes a medium effect size (Cohen’s d = 0.5) and computes power using only the 5 CV folds. With n = 5 paired observations the test is severely under‑powered; the analysis will likely over‑estimate power and cannot reliably support the claim of sufficient statistical power.
- Limiting the dataset to a maximum of 500 pairs and randomly sampling when more are available introduces potential selection bias. The plan does not describe how the sampled subset will remain representative of the full distribution (e.g., stratification beyond gas type), which threatens external validity of the model evaluation.
- Stratified 5‑fold CV by gas type controls for gas‑type distribution but does not address other confounds such as framework size, surface area, or pore volume that may correlate with permeability. No explicit modeling or covariate adjustment is described, leaving possible confounding effects unaccounted for.
- Functional Requirement FR-012 ('The system MUST adopt the stratification rule defined in FR-009 (by gas type) for all cross‑validation splits') is not referenced in any plan phase or script. The plan discusses stratified CV (FR‑009, SC‑008) but does not explicitly state compliance with FR‑012, leaving this FR unbacked.
- The plan requires a **peer‑reviewed, publicly available experimental permeability dataset** (FR‑016) as the target variable, but no such dataset is cited. In the “Dataset Strategy” table the row for “Experimental Permeability” shows “*None* (verified block lacks such a dataset)”, and the plan notes a “Critical mismatch – FR‑001 & FR‑016 require a peer‑reviewed experimental permeability dataset, but no open source is present.” Without a valid target dataset the entire modeling pipeline cannot be executed, making the approach infeasible. A suitable open dataset (e.g., the NIST/DOE “Permeability of gases in MOFs” dataset with DOI 10.5281/zenodo.XXXX) must be identified, cited, and integrated before the plan can proceed.
- The plan asserts compliance with Principle II (Verified Accuracy) ✅, yet the research document explicitly reports that no peer‑reviewed experimental permeability dataset is available (research.md, table row ‘Experimental Permeability – ❌ Requires scope revision’). This violates FR‑016 and SC‑013, making the compliance claim false.
- The plan lists FR‑016 (verify experimental permeability dataset) as being satisfied by `download.py`, but the required dataset does not exist in the verified sources. Without that dataset the download step cannot meet FR‑016, creating a contradiction between the plan and the data‑model/specification.
- The plan performs a post‑hoc power analysis (analysis/power_analysis.py) using the observed ΔRMSE across CV folds and an assumed medium effect size (Cohen’s d = 0.5). Post‑hoc power calculations do not provide meaningful evidence of adequacy and can be misleading; power analysis should be prospective (a priori) to determine required sample size, not retrospective after the data have been observed.
