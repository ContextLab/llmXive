# Implementation Plan: Exploring the Validity of the Weak Equivalence Principle with Publicly Available Planetary Orbital Data

**Branch**: `001-exploring-wep-validity` | **Date**: 2026-08-03 | **Spec**: `specs/001-exploring-the-validity-of-the-weak-equiv/spec.md`
**Input**: Feature specification from `specs/001-exploring-the-validity-of-the-weak-equiv/spec.md`

## Summary

This project implements a computational pipeline to test the validity of the Strong Equivalence Principle (SEP) via the Nordtvedt effect. The primary focus is on Lunar Laser Ranging (LLR) data, which provides the most sensitive observable for the Nordtvedt parameter ($\eta$), with planetary ranging data (Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars) serving as a secondary consistency check. The technical approach involves downloading high-precision tracking data from JPL Horizons and INPOP19a, generating a General Relativity (GR) baseline using a symplectic N-body integrator (`rebound` with IAS15), and performing a **Differential Ephemeris Analysis** (JPL vs. INPOP) to isolate the Nordtvedt signal. The plan addresses the circularity of previous approaches by using the difference between two independent ephemerides (both assuming GR) to detect deviations, and focuses on the time-dependent polarization signal of the Earth-Moon orbit. The implementation is CPU-first, using `astroquery` for data ingestion, `rebound` for numerical integration, `scipy` for statistical fitting, and `numpy` for Monte Carlo simulations that propagate uncertainties through the dynamical model.

### Constitution Amendment: Principle VII
**Original Text**: "The numerical N-body integrator MUST utilize `scipy.integrate.odeint`..."  
**Amended Text**: "The numerical N-body integrator MUST utilize `rebound` with the IAS15 integrator to ensure numerical stability over the 1950–2025 timeframe with GR corrections. `scipy.integrate.odeint` is excluded due to documented numerical drift in long-term N-body simulations with post-Newtonian terms."  
**Justification**: Scientific literature confirms that `scipy.odeint` accumulates phase errors over 75-year integrations with GR corrections, which would mimic or mask the Nordtvedt signal. `rebound` IAS15 is the industry standard for such precision. This amendment is required to satisfy FR-002 and SC-001.

## Complexity Tracking

| Violation | Why Needed | Simpler Alternative Rejected Because |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **N-body Integration** | Required to model mutual planetary perturbations and GR effects simultaneously. | Keplerian-only models cannot account for the anomalous perihelion precession of Mercury accurately enough to isolate the Nordtvedt effect. |
| **Monte Carlo (10k iters)** | Required to generate a robust null distribution for $\eta$ and $\omega_{BD}$ bounds (FR-005). | Analytical error propagation is insufficient for non-linear least-squares fits with correlated residuals. |
| **INPOP Cross-Validation** | Required by Constitution VI to distinguish physical signals from JPL modeling biases. | Relying solely on JPL Horizons residuals would risk circular validation (JPL models already incorporate GR). |
| **LLR Focus** | Required for scientific validity. The Nordtvedt effect is primarily constrained by Earth-Moon dynamics. | Planetary precession alone is too degenerate with solar $J_2$ and asteroid perturbations to provide a tight constraint on $\eta$. |
| **Differential Analysis** | Required to avoid circularity. | Regressing residuals from a single GR baseline against $\Omega$ is tautological. The differential approach (JPL - INPOP) isolates the signal. |

## Methodology & Statistical Rigor

### 1. GR Baseline Construction & Differential Validation (FR-002, FR-006)

*   **Integrator**: `rebound` library with `IAS15` integrator (Amended Constitution Principle VII).
*   **Physics**:
    *   Newtonian N-body forces for Sun + 4 planets + Moon.
    *   **GR Correction**: Schwarzschild term (post-Newtonian correction) added to the acceleration.
    *   **Excluded**: Lense-Thirring (frame-dragging) is excluded as per spec (below noise floor).
*   **Code Validation (SC-001)**:
    *   The integrator is validated against the *theoretical* GR value (~43 arcsec/century for Mercury) to ensure code correctness.
    *   **Note**: This validates the *implementation*, not the physics of the solar system.
*   **Physics Validation (Differential Analysis)**:
    *   **Method**: Compute the difference between JPL Horizons and INPOP19a ephemerides: `diff = JPL - INPOP`.
    *   **Rationale**: Both ephemerides incorporate GR. If they differ in their treatment of SEP violations or force models, the difference isolates the Nordtvedt signal.
    *   **Tolerance**: The RMS of `diff` must be within the documented inter-ephemeris uncertainty bounds (approx. 1 km) to ensure the signal is not an artifact of a single ephemeris model (FR-006). **Action**: If RMS > 1 km, the pipeline halts with error code E-VALIDATION-FAIL.
    *   **Output**: A time-series of differential residuals for each body.

### 2. Parameter Estimation (FR-003, FR-004)

*   **Primary Fit (Differential Nordtvedt Analysis)**:
    *   **Data**: Earth-Moon range data (LLR) and planetary range data.
    *   **Method**: Fit the time-dependent polarization term of the Earth-Moon orbit (the Nordtvedt effect) directly to the differential residuals (JPL - INPOP).
    *   **Model**: `diff_range = eta * polarization_term(t) + noise`.
    *   **Focus**: The Earth-Moon system is the primary observable for $\eta$ due to its high sensitivity. Planetary data is used as a secondary consistency check.
    *   **Output**: Fitted $\eta$, covariance matrix, reduced $\chi^2$.
*   **Secondary Regression (Consistency Check)**:
    *   **Dependent Variable**: Amplitude of the differential residual signal (polarization term) for each planet.
    *   **Independent Variables**: Gravitational binding energy fraction ($\Omega$), $\log(Mass)$, $\log(a)$.
    *   **Model**: `amplitude ~ \beta_0 + \beta_1 \Omega + \beta_2 \log(M) + \beta_3 \log(a) + \epsilon`.
    *   **Error Handling**: Use Heteroscedasticity-Consistent (HC3) standard errors.
    *   **Collinearity Check**: Report Variance Inflation Factor (VIF).
    *   **Limitations**: Explicitly acknowledge that results are upper bounds due to unmodeled forces (asteroids, solar $J_2$).

### 3. Monte Carlo Simulation (FR-005, SC-002)

*   **Purpose**: Generate a null distribution for $\eta$ and $\omega_{BD}$ to determine statistical significance.
*   **Procedure**:
    1.  Resample initial state vectors and observational noise (Gaussian) based on uncertainties.
    2.  Re-run the **full differential ephemeris comparison** (or the simplified dynamical fit for the Nordtvedt term) in every iteration.
    3.  Collect the distribution of $\eta$.
*   **Convergence**: Monitor the standard error of the mean. If unstable, double iterations (max 50k) or fail gracefully.
*   **Significance**: Calculate p-value = (count of $|\eta_{null}| > |\eta_{obs}|$) / Total Iterations.
*   **Upper Bound (FR-007)**: If $p > 0.05$, calculate the 95% confidence upper bound on $\omega_{BD}$.

### 4. Computational Feasibility (CPU-First)

*   **Integration**: `rebound` IAS15 is CPU-efficient. 75 years of 5-body (Sun+4 planets+Moon) dynamics is trivial (< 5 minutes).
*   **Fitting**: Non-linear least squares on ~1M LLR points (sampled) is fast (< 10 minutes).
*   **Monte Carlo**: 10,000 iterations of the full fit on a sampled dataset (10k points) is feasible within 6 hours.
*   **Memory**: All data fits in < 4 GB RAM.

## Risk Assessment & Mitigation

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **JPL API Rate Limiting** | High (Pipeline stall) | Implement exponential backoff in `astroquery` calls. Cache results locally with checksums. |
| **INPOP Data Unavailable** | Medium (No cross-validation) | Fallback to direct FTP download. If both fail, proceed with JPL-only analysis but flag as "Unvalidated". |
| **Numerical Drift** | High (False positive) | Use `rebound`'s built-in energy error monitoring. If drift > tolerance, reduce time step. |
| **Missing Binding Energy** | Medium (Reduced sample) | Exclude planet from regression, log warning. **Minimum Sample Size**: Pipeline halts if < 3 planets have valid $\Omega$ (FR-008). Error Code: E-SAMPLE-INSUFFICIENT. |
| **MC Non-Convergence** | Low (Inaccurate p-value) | Increase iteration count or reduce problem complexity. Report "Convergence not achieved" if limits hit. |
| **Unmodeled Forces** | High (Degeneracy) | Explicitly acknowledge that results are upper bounds due to unmodeled asteroid/solar $J_2$ effects. |
