## Research-question validation

### Phenomenon-vs-method check

**Verdict**: pass

The question asks about the causal relationship between specific linguistic frames ("battle," "journey") and public psychological attitudes (stigma, help-seeking), which is a substantive inquiry into human cognition and social perception. The mention of "controlled vignette experiments" and "discourse sources" describes the methodology for testing the hypothesis rather than defining the research question itself as a performance benchmark for those methods.

### Circularity check

**Verdict**: pass

The predictor (metaphorical framing in experimental vignettes or text corpora) and the predicted variable (stigma scores via CAMI scale or sentiment analysis) are derived from independent measurement streams. The experimental design explicitly separates the stimulus (the text) from the outcome measure (the psychometric scale), and the discourse analysis uses external NLP models to score sentiment, avoiding the mechanical guarantee where the input text directly dictates the output metric.

### Triviality check

**Verdict**: pass

While "war" metaphors are often anecdotally associated with stigma, a null result (finding no difference) would be scientifically valuable by suggesting that core clinical details or individual differences outweigh framing effects. Conversely, a positive result would provide the empirical evidence needed to guide public health communication strategies, making either outcome informative for the field.

### Question-narrowing check

**Verdict**: pass

The question names a specific domain relationship (the impact of metaphorical framing on treatment attitudes) rather than an implementation constraint like "Can a specific NLP model detect these frames within 1 hour?" The focus remains on the psychological phenomenon of how language shapes attitude, not on the technical capabilities of the measurement tools.

### Overall verdict

**Verdict**: validated

All four checks pass; the research question clearly targets a substantive psychological phenomenon with independent predictors and outcomes, and the potential results would be informative regardless of direction. The project is ready to advance to project initialization without requiring a reframing of the core question.
