# Feature Specification: Extending Zone of Proximal Policy Optimization

This specification outlines the requirements for extending the ZPPO methodology with a Confidence-Adaptive Pruning (CAP) mechanism.

## Objectives
1. Implement a static baseline simulation (US1).
2. Implement a dynamic CAP-based simulation (US2).
3. Perform statistical comparison between the two (US3).

## Key Concepts
- **ZPPO**: Zone of Proximal Policy Optimization
- **CAP**: Confidence-Adaptive Pruning
- **NCQ**: Negative Candidate-included Question
- **AUCC**: Area Under the Convergence Curve
