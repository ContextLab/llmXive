# Research Context

## Background
Climate change poses significant risks to smallholder agriculture in Sub-Saharan Africa. Climate-Smart Agriculture (CSA) practices are promoted to enhance resilience, but their impact on yield stability remains underexplored.

## Research Gap
Existing literature focuses on average yield effects, neglecting yield variability. Financial access may confound the relationship between CSA adoption and outcomes.

## Objectives
1. Quantify the association between CSA practices and yield stability
2. Control for financial access using HFIAS
3. Assess robustness across cloud cover thresholds

## Expected Contributions
- Evidence on CSA effectiveness for stability (not just mean yield)
- Methodological framework for spatial-temporal analysis
- Policy insights for targeted interventions
