# Methodology Notes

## Vibrational Energy Definition
Provisional Formula (Unverified Source):
$E_{vib} = m \cdot \text{var}(a) \cdot (\Delta t)^2$

Where:
- $m$: Particle mass (kg)
- $a$: Acceleration (m/s²)
- $\Delta t$: Time step (s)
- $\text{var}(a)$: Variance of acceleration over a window.

Units: $kg \cdot (m/s^2)^2 \cdot s^2 = kg \cdot m^2/s^2 = Joules$.
