# Research: Investigating the Impact of Network Structure on Energy Dissipation in Driven Oscillators

## Summary of Research

This research validates the feasibility of simulating coupled harmonic oscillators on synthetic network topologies to correlate structural metrics with energy dissipation rates. The study relies on standard numerical methods (`scipy.integrate.solve_ivp`) and graph theory libraries (`networkx`), ensuring CPU-tractability and reproducibility. The statistical approach uses **Partial Least Squares (PLS) regression** to handle collinearity and identify specific structural drivers, validated against a **null model (permutation test)** to ensure non-triviality.

## Theoretical Background

### Network Topologies
- **Random (Erdős-Rényi)**: Each edge exists with probability $p$. Expected clustering coefficient $C \approx p$.
- **Scale-Free (Barabási-Albert)**: Power-law degree distribution $P(k) \sim k^{-\gamma}$. Expected $\gamma \in [2.0, 3.0]$.
- **Small-World (Watts-Strogatz)**: High clustering, short average path length. Generated by rewiring a regular lattice.
- **Lattice**: Regular grid structure; high clustering, long path length.
- **Star**: One central hub connected to all others; low clustering, short path length for leaves.

### Driven Damped Oscillators
The system is modeled by:
$$ m \ddot{x}_i + \gamma \dot{x}_i + k \sum_{j} A_{ij} (x_i - x_j) = F_i(t) $$
Where $A_{ij}$ is the adjacency matrix, $\gamma$ is damping, and $F_i(t)$ is the driving force (active for $t \in [0, 100]$).
Energy decay rate $\lambda$ is extracted from $E(t) = A e^{-\lambda t} \cos(\omega t + \phi) + C$ for $t > 100$.
**Validation**: The solver is validated against the analytical eigenvalues of the Laplacian matrix for a known small graph (e.g., ring) to ensure network-dependent dissipation modes are correctly captured, not just the single-oscillator limit.

## Methodological Rigor

### Statistical Methods
- **Partial Least Squares (PLS) Regression**: Chosen over PCR to maximize covariance between predictors and response, enabling the calculation of **Variable Importance in Projection (VIP)** scores. This allows direct interpretation of which structural features drive dissipation.
- **Multiple Comparison Correction**: Bonferroni or Holm-Bonferroni correction applied to all p-values to control family-wise error rate.
- **Sensitivity Analysis**: Thresholds for significance (p < 0.01, 0.05, 0.1) are swept to test robustness.
- **Collinearity Check**: Variance Inflation Factor (VIF) calculated; if VIF > 5, results are framed descriptively. PLS inherently handles collinearity, but VIF is reported for diagnostics.
- **Null Model Validation**: A permutation test (shuffling topology-to-decay mapping [deferred] times) is performed. The observed correlation must exceed a high percentile of the null distribution to be considered non-trivial, addressing the risk of tautological results.

### Numerical Stability
- **Convergence Testing**: 10+ random seeds used for a representative topology to ensure decay rate variance < 1% of mean.
- **Solver Validation**: `solve_ivp` with `RK45` tested against:
    1. Analytical solution for a single oscillator (λ proportional to γ).
    2. **Analytical eigenvalues of the Laplacian** for a known small graph (e.g., ring) to verify network-dependent dissipation modes.
- **Resonance Handling**: Instances with negative decay rates (energy growth) flagged as "resonant" and excluded from regression.

### Power Analysis & Limitations
- **Sample Size**: A sufficient sample size is the minimum feasible for regression with 5 predictors.
- **MDES Assumption**: Power analysis assumes a medium effect size (f² = 0.15) based on pilot simulations.
- **Limitation**: Power may be low for small effect sizes; results will be interpreted as **exploratory/hypothesis-generating** rather than confirmatory. Type II errors are a primary constraint.

## Decision Rationale

- **CPU-First**: All methods are CPU-tractable; no GPU escape hatch needed.
- **Synthetic Data**: Ensures full control over variables and reproducibility.
- **PLS vs PCR**: PLS chosen to handle inevitable collinearity while preserving interpretability via VIP scores, directly answering the research question.
- **Null Model**: Essential to prove that observed correlations are not artifacts of the data generation process (tautology risk).
- **Solver Validation**: Validating against Laplacian eigenvalues ensures the simulation captures the core physics of coupled networks, not just isolated oscillators.

## Assumptions & Limitations

- **Model Validity**: Synthetic networks and ODEs accurately reflect the physics of interest.
- **Inference Framing**: Results are "simulation-causal" (valid within the synthetic universe due to controlled randomization) but not "real-world-causal".
- **Power**: A limited sample size may limit detection of subtle effects; study is exploratory.
- **Thresholds**: p < 0.05 is standard; sensitivity analysis tests robustness.
- **Tautology Risk**: Mitigated by null model validation.
