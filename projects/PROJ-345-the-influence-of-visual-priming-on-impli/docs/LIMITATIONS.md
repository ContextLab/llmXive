# Limitations

## Observational Nature
This study is observational in nature. The findings are associational and do not imply causality.

## Derived Prime Valence
Prime valence scores are derived using a VAD regression model. While this model is optimized for CPU inference, it may not capture all nuances of human perception.

## Human-Rated Ambiguity
The analysis relies on human-rated ambiguity scores. If these scores are incomplete or biased, the results may be affected.

## Sample Size
The study is limited by the sample size of the available IAT datasets. Small sample sizes may reduce the power of the statistical tests.

## Data Quality
The system halts if data quality thresholds are not met (e.g., >10% missing images). This ensures high-quality analysis but may limit the amount of data available.

## Generalizability
The findings are specific to the stimuli and participants in the dataset. Generalizability to other populations or stimuli is not guaranteed.
