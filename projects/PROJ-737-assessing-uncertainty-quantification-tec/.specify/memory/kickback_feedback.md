## Unresolved concerns

- The plan substitutes the intended Elastic Modulus dataset with Formation Energy (proxy) (see Phase 1 description). Formation Energy measures thermodynamic stability, not mechanical stiffness, so the construct being evaluated (UQ for mechanical‑property predictions) is not represented. This weakens the claim that the study spans electronic, mechanical, and thermal domains.
- Power analysis notes an estimated power of ≈ 0.30 for a medium effect size with n = 200 test samples (see Statistical Analysis Plan). Such low power makes it unlikely to detect meaningful differences, especially after Bonferroni correction, undermining the ability to draw reliable conclusions about method superiority.
- Baseline models differ across UQ techniques (GPR uses a GaussianProcessRegressor, MC‑Dropout and Deep Ensembles use a small MLP, Split‑Conformal uses XGBoost). This introduces a confound: performance differences may stem from model architecture rather than the UQ method itself. A fair comparison requires a common underlying predictive model for all UQ approaches.
- The plan downloads the *Formation Energy* dataset as a proxy for the required *Elastic Modulus* dataset. Specification FR‑001 and success criterion SC‑005 explicitly require an open Elastic Modulus dataset (originally from Materials Project). Using Formation Energy (a thermodynamic property) does not provide the mechanical‑property uncertainty the research question targets, violating the dataset‑appropriateness requirement.
- The plan states “Emits five CSV artefacts that conform to schemas in `contracts/`”, but the Quickstart and the contracts directory define six artefacts (`per_sample_errors.csv`, `metrics_raw.csv`, `statistical_report.csv`, `sensitivity_report.csv`, `uq_methods.csv`, `pipeline_log.csv`). This count mismatch violates the functional requirement that the plan accurately describe its outputs.
- The plan never mentions any of the entities defined in `data-model.md` (MaterialsDataset, UQMethod, PerSampleResult, EvaluationMetric, StatisticalTestResult, PipelineExecution). According to the cross‑doc rule, every data‑model entity must be named (and used) by the plan, and every plan step must reference an entity that actually exists.
- The Constitution Check table lists Principles I, III, VI, VII but omits Principle II (silent fallbacks) and Principle V (non‑real‑call testing). While not a hard violation, the omission makes the check incomplete and could be interpreted as a missing SSoT designation for fallback behavior.
- The plan proposes applying paired Wilcoxon signed‑rank tests to per‑sample Calibration Error (ECE) and Sharpness vectors across methods. However, both Calibration Error (as defined) and Sharpness are scalar summary statistics computed per method‑dataset pair, not per‑sample quantities. Consequently there are no paired observations for the Wilcoxon test, rendering the statistical comparison invalid. The analysis should either (a) compute per‑sample quantities suitable for paired testing (e.g., absolute prediction errors, interval widths, binary coverage indicators) or (b) use an appropriate method for comparing scalar metrics across methods such as bootstrap confidence intervals or permutation tests.
