### Recommendations

1. **Standard Practice**: α=0.05 remains the conventional threshold for most
 neuroimaging studies, balancing Type I and Type II error rates.

2. **Exploratory Analyses**: For hypothesis-generating research, α=0.10 may
 be appropriate to increase sensitivity, with appropriate caveats.

3. **Confirmatory Studies**: For high-stakes replication or clinical
 applications, α=0.01 provides more stringent control of false positives.

4. **Smoothing Kernel Effects**: The analysis indicates that smoothing kernel
 choice interacts with alpha threshold, suggesting that preprocessing
 parameters should be considered alongside statistical thresholds.
