The task lacks a mechanism to ensure 3+ valid runs exist (given the plan's 3-seed budget vs spec's 5-seed requirement), meaning the project may be forced to violate the Constitution by design without a clear resolution path in the task.

However, T018c is marked as a single task that 'iterates through 5 seeds... calling T018b and T018d'. If T018c is the orchestrator, it should logically be the entry point that *calls* the implementations in T018b/T018d, not a task that *depends* on their completion in a linear sense if they are meant to be run in parallel seeds. More critically, T018c is marked as dependent on T018b and T018d, but T018b and T018d are implementation tasks. The dependency chain implies T018c cannot start until T018b and T018d are *implemented*, which is correct, but the [P] tag is missing on T018a/T018b/T018d where appropriate, and the 'Sequential Logic' note contradicts the parallel potential of T018b/T018d execution.
