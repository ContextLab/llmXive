# Tasks: Neural Correlates of Simulated Social Exclusion on Default Mode Network Dynamics

**Inputs**: `spec.md`, `plan.md`, `research.md`, `data-model.md`, contracts, and existing artifacts.
All paths are relative to the project directory. All computation is CPU‑only (2 cores, ~7 GB RAM, ~14 GB disk, ≤6 h); no synthetic or placeholder data are ever generated — every metric derives from real OpenNeuro recordings and event timings.
