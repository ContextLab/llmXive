## Unresolved concerns

- The plan measures musical preference using raw listening minutes (log‑transformed) rather than a proportion of total listening time. This conflates preference with overall activity level, threatening construct validity of the ‘preference’ variable.
- Confounding by total listening activity is not addressed. Users who listen more overall will have higher raw minutes across all genres, potentially inflating correlations. The methodology should include a control for total listening minutes (e.g., using listening proportion or including total minutes as a covariate).
- No a priori power analysis is provided. While ~200 k records are anticipated, the plan does not justify that this sample size is sufficient to detect the effect sizes of interest (e.g., r ≈ 0.1). Including a power calculation would strengthen methodological rigor.
- The plan does not specify checks for the assumptions underlying Pearson correlation and OLS regression (e.g., linearity, normality of residuals, homoscedasticity). Without diagnostic checks, the validity of the statistical inferences may be compromised.
- The plan states that the OpenML BFI‑2 personality dataset and the Last.fm public listening archive will be merged on a common user identifier. These two datasets are unrelated and do not share a common user key, making a deterministic merge impossible. This constitutes a wrong‑dataset choice for the research question, which requires a dataset that contains both personality scores and music‑listening behavior for the same participants (e.g., a study‑collected dataset or a platform‑specific dataset that links user profiles to listening histories).
- The plan’s contract‑validation section only references `processed_dataset.schema.yaml` and `analysis_results.csv` contracts. It omits the `analysis_output.schema.yaml`, `results.schema.yaml`, and `report.schema.yaml` contracts that are defined in the `contracts/` directory and required for the final report and analysis output validation.
- The Constitution Check lists Principle V (Versioning Discipline) but does not explicitly reference the `state/projects/...yaml` artifact where hashes are stored, nor does it mention the `pyproject.toml` (only `requirements.txt`). This leaves a minor ambiguity about the single source of truth for versioning.
- The plan assumes a direct merge of the OpenML BFI‑2 personality dataset with the Last.fm listening archive on a common `user_id` (see “merge them on a common user identifier”). In reality these two public datasets contain disjoint participant pools with no guaranteed overlapping identifiers, making the merge invalid and rendering any subsequent correlation or regression analysis meaningless.
