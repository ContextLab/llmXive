1. PURPOSE OF THE STUDY
You are invited to participate in a research study investigating how the visual design of websites influences your perception of their credibility. The goal is to understand the relationship between aesthetic quality and trust in online information.

2. PROCEDURES
If you agree to participate, you will be asked to:
- Read and acknowledge this consent form.
- View four different versions of a webpage containing neutral text.
- Rate each version on scales of credibility and professionalism.
- Provide basic demographic information (Age, Education level).
The entire session will take approximately 10-15 minutes.

3. RISKS AND DISCOMFORTS
There are no known risks greater than those encountered in everyday life. The study involves viewing website designs and answering questions.

4. BENEFITS
There is no direct benefit to you for participating. However, the results may contribute to a better understanding of online trust and design principles.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY
Your responses will be recorded anonymously. Your IP address will be hashed immediately upon capture and stored only as a cryptographic hash to detect duplicate submissions. The raw IP address will never be stored. Your demographic data and ratings will be associated with a unique participant ID.

6. VOLUNTARY PARTICIPATION
Your participation is entirely voluntary. You may choose not to participate or may withdraw at any time without penalty. If you withdraw, your data will be discarded if possible.

7. CONTACT INFORMATION
If you have questions about this study, please contact the research team at [Contact Email].

8. CONSENT
By clicking "I Agree", you confirm that you have read this form, understand the information, and voluntarily agree to participate.
