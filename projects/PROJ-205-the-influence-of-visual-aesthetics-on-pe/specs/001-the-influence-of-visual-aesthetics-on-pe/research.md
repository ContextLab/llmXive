# Research Background

## Hypothesis
H1: Participants will rate content presented in a "Professional" visual style as significantly more credible than the same content presented in "Low-Quality" or "Neutral" styles.

## Theoretical Framework
This study is grounded in the "Halo Effect" in web design, where users generalize their perception of a site's visual appeal to its content quality and credibility (Fogg et al., 2003).

## Variables
- **Independent Variable**: Visual Aesthetic Condition (4 levels: Professional, Minimalist, Low-Quality, Neutral).
- **Dependent Variables**:
 1. Perceived Credibility (1-7 Likert).
 2. Perceived Professionalism (1-7 Likert).
- **Control Variables**: Text content (identical across conditions), Order of presentation (Latin Square).
- **Covariates**: Age, Education Level.

## Power Analysis
Assuming a medium effect size (f = 0.25), alpha = 0.05, power = 0.80, and correlation among repeated measures = 0.5, a sample size of approximately 128 is required.
