## Research-question validation

### Phenomenon-vs-method check

**Verdict**: pass

The question asks whether quasar absorption‑line spectra reveal a genuine spatial or temporal variation in the fine‑structure constant, a fundamental physics phenomenon. It does not hinge on the performance of a particular algorithm or hardware configuration, only on proper statistical treatment of systematics.

### Circularity check

**Verdict**: pass

Both the predictor (the observed wavelengths in the spectra) and the predicted variable (Δα/α) are derived from the same raw data, but the analysis is a hypothesis test rather than a regression where one variable predicts another. The relationship is not mechanically guaranteed; it depends on the Bayesian model linking line shifts to α variation.

### Triviality check

**Verdict**: pass

A statistically significant detection would have profound implications for fundamental physics, while a robust null result would tighten constraints on new physics. Both outcomes would be publishable and informative.

### Question-narrowing check

**Verdict**: pass

The question focuses on a domain relationship—possible spatial/temporal variation of α—rather than on constraints of a specific implementation or computational budget.

**Verdict**: validated
