# Annotation Instructions: Perceived Authenticity and Hedge Identification

## 1. Overview
You are tasked with annotating a series of short text turns (chatbot responses or dialogue snippets).
Your goal is to provide two types of labels for each turn:
1. **Perceived Authenticity**: A rating of how authentic the turn feels.
2. **Hedge Identification**: The specific word indices where uncertainty markers (hedges) appear.

## 2. Authenticity Rating Scale (1-5)
Please rate each turn on a scale of 1 to 5 based on your intuitive perception of its authenticity.

* **1 (Not Authentic)**: The text feels robotic, overly formal, deceptive, or clearly generated by a machine without human nuance.
* **2 (Slightly Authentic)**: The text feels somewhat artificial or stiff, but has minor natural elements.
* **3 (Moderately Authentic)**: The text feels neutral; it could be human or AI, with no strong signals either way.
* **4 (Mostly Authentic)**: The text feels natural, conversational, and human-like, with minor artificial traits.
* **5 (Very Authentic)**: The text feels genuinely human, nuanced, and emotionally resonant. It exhibits high levels of natural language flow.

**Focus**: Do not rate based on the *truthfulness* of the statement, but rather the *style* and *tone* that make it feel "human" or "authentic."

## 3. Hedge Identification
Hedges are words or phrases that express uncertainty, caution, or lack of commitment (e.g., "maybe", "perhaps", "I think", "seems").

**Task**: For each turn, identify the **word indices** (0-based) where a hedge appears.

**Example**:
Text: "I think maybe we should go."
Tokens: ["I", "think", "maybe", "we", "should", "go."]
Indices: 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5

* "think" is a hedge (Index 1)
* "maybe" is a hedge (Index 2)

**Input Format**: Enter the indices separated by spaces (e.g., `1 2`). If there are no hedges, enter `none`.

## 4. Procedure
1. Read the text provided.
2. Assign an Authenticity Score (1-5).
3. Identify Hedge word indices.
4. Submit your input.
5. Proceed to the next turn.

## 5. Important Notes
* **Consistency**: Try to apply the criteria consistently across all 50 turns.
* **No Guessing**: If you are unsure, trust your first intuition.
* **Real Data**: These are real conversation snippets. Do not assume they are all AI-generated or all human.
