# Research scope inflation (2026-10-09)

The live totient canary's original idea explicitly requests one exact analysis, a small test module, 8–12 research tasks, 12 (N,p) combinations, counts/CSV/JSON, figures, and a reproducible paper. The generated specification acquired 17 functional requirements and 12 success criteria. These include exact internal variable names (FR-014), duplicate timing-file requirements, exact error typography, and a zero-conditional-denominator CLI case. For valid N>=1, phi(1)=1 contributes a nonzero residue for every supported prime, so that CLI case cannot occur. The tasks nevertheless require an offending CLI argument for it. This is an upstream requirements problem, not a missing implementation.

The specifier prompt previously required three stories, at least five FRs and three SCs, and default numeric targets; it also called every sensitivity sweep CPU-trivial. Remove those inflation incentives. Retain measurable acceptance, explicit scientific validation, and the idea's bounds. Scope and testability review now call out unnecessary engineering and unreachable inputs. Task reviewers distinguish future unchecked outputs from unsupported completion claims.
